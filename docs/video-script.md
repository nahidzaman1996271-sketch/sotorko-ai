# Sotorko AI — Pitch Video Voice Script (Team Cady Crush)

**How to record**
- Record **one file per scene** (11 files), named `01-open`, `02-team`, … as in the headings below. Phone voice memo is fine (m4a, mp3 or wav).
- Use a quiet room, hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, and don't touch it while talking.
- **Pause about 1 second between lines** (each line below is one line). The animations sync to those pauses.
- Speak calmly, a little slower than normal. Re-record any scene you stumble on; you don't need to redo the whole video.
- The target time is a guide only. Within ±3 seconds is fine.

---

## 01-open (about 8 s)
Every monsoon, the rivers of Bangladesh rise.
The forecast already knows.
The people in the water's path, often don't.

## 02-team (about 25 s)
We are team Cady Crush, from Daffodil International University.
I'm Nahid Ibn Zaman, the team leader. I lead development and the backend.
Md. Rasel Hasan Sabuj builds our agent orchestration and messaging.
Md Tusher Hossen builds the flood-risk model and the Bangla language AI.
And Md. Ha-Mim Ferdowsh builds the data pipeline and low-cost river sensors.

## 03-problem (about 11 s)
In August 2024, floods in eastern Bangladesh hit 5.8 million people.
More than half a million fled to shelters.
Over two million children were at risk.

## 04-gap (about 11 s)
The forecast existed.
But warnings are technical bulletins, often in English, on websites.
And when the water rose, 92 percent of mobile towers in Feni went dark.

## 05-meet (about 10 s)
Meet Sotorko AI.
Sotorko means "alert".
A team of AI agents that turns forecasts into action,
in Bangla, before the water arrives.

## 06-agents (about 27 s)
A Monitor agent watches river levels and rainfall, every fifteen minutes.
A Risk agent scores every union, and estimates the hours to impact.
A Bangla Communicator writes a clear alert, and sends it by SMS, WhatsApp, or voice call.
A Shelter Router sends families to a shelter that still has room.
And an Orchestrator plans, checks the results, and asks a human officer to approve every severe alert.

## 07-journey (about 12 s)
Meet Rahima, in Feni.
Thirty-three hours before the flood, her basic phone rings.
A calm Bangla voice tells her where to go, and which shelter has space.
She presses one. Safe.

## 08-loop (about 12 s)
Her neighbour replies: "stuck, five people, on the roof."
Rescue Triage reads the Bangla message, marks it critical, and sends the nearest boat.
Anyone who stays silent is called again.

## 09-tech (about 15 s)
Under the hood:
a large language model with tool calling plans each step.
A gradient-boosted model, trained on river and rainfall history, predicts the risk.
BanglaBERT reads the replies,
and Bangla text-to-speech makes the calls.

## 10-impact (about 14 s)
Alerts are always free for citizens.
Districts subscribe, NGOs fund early action, and telecom partners subsidise the messages.
The core is open source, ready for cyclones and landslides next.

## 11-close (about 10 s)
First, we replay the 2024 Feni flood.
Then, we pilot in one upazila of Sylhet.
Sotorko AI.
The forecast, delivered.
